# The bench's meeting: a script written for the purpose. Nobody said these words; the voices
# that say them are synthesised. It is in French because that is what the product hears.
#
# Two sides, as a session has two tracks: `local` is a room with one microphone and two people
# in it; `remote` is two people on the call. A line is a turn: who speaks, the silence in
# seconds between the end of the turn before and the start of this one — negative, the turn
# starts before the other has finished: an overlap — and what is said.

voice claire local
voice marc local
voice sophie remote
voice julien remote

julien  -0.6  Quatre jours, ce n'est pas grand-chose si le support est prêt.

julien   0.4  Je peux faire passer les entretiens techniques la semaine du deux novembre.
