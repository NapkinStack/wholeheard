## 2. Five readings

1. **I** **The leaders are leaving the job.** Granola adds dictation and briefs, Otter sales agents,
   Notion one-click "skills". Each widens away from *the minutes of this meeting, trusted*. That is
   the ground left to hold.
2. **F** **The citation is becoming normal** (Notion's hover and click; Granola's magnifier, review
   of 2026-10-26). **I** A cloud tool that deletes the audio stops at the text. Wholeheard keeps the
   audio as recorded (ADR-0004): it alone can play the second a decision was taken.
3. **I** **Notice has become the legal front, and defaults its target** (§3). The start reminder,
   on by default, and *Copier l'annonce* (D50) stand on the right side of it.
4. **F, read below** **Whisper is no longer the accuracy leader in French on read speech** (§5), and
   **F** a local rival already ships the alternative. **I** The defects our real call shows —
   inventions on silence, loops — belong to Whisper's way of decoding, so a change of engine may
   do what no heuristic was allowed to (the decider's rule of 2026-10-17, no tuning per recording).
5. **F** **The calm interface is what admired tools now claim**: "Don't compete for attention you
   haven't earned", "Structure should be felt not seen"
   ([Linear, 2026-03-12](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh), *vendor*). **U** The
   general "UX trends 2026" pages found are agency marketing with no data; none is used here.

**What the framer infers, and a lawyer has not read:**

- **I** Holding nothing and training on nothing removes the claims aimed at a vendor's use of the
  data. It removes nothing of the user's own duty: the charter's constraint stands.
- **I** Never computing a voiceprint keeps Wholeheard outside the theory of BIPA's counts: one more
  reason to refuse automatic speaker identification, beyond its cost.
- **I** The reminder that "a setting removes" is the exposure to re-read before strangers install
  it. An opt-in notice is what is attacked; this one is opt-out, and it reaches the user, not the
  participants. *Copier l'annonce* is what bridges that. See cycle 06's open questions.
