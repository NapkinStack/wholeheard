# D59 — a meeting's context, drawn before it is built

**Drawn** 2026-10-27, first round · **Status**: drawn by D59's author, shown to the decider with its pull request ([cycle 04, D59](../../cycles/04-wholeheard-on-macos.md#d59)) · **Frozen**: these drawings are not kept up to date. What the decider accepts becomes the criteria of the deliverable that builds it; once built, the window and its screenshots are the truth. A new round is a new folder.

The words are proposals, in French; the yellow notes are the author's proposals for the decider.

| # | Board | What the user sees, and can do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The context](1-the-context-light.png) · [dark](1-the-context-dark.png) | While recording, in the small window at its size (380 × 400): *Ajouter le contexte*, closed by default, opens a field of two lines and its count, 400 characters at most, *Arrêter* never moving; another script than Latin or Cyrillic said in the count's line. In the full window, the page of the meeting being recorded holds the same field, open. On a meeting's page, the context shown and written; changed after its transcript or minutes were made, *Refaire avec ce contexte*; closed, and said, while either is made or a transcript is left unfinished. The agreement before a first sending (board 5) unchanged |

[`board.html`](board.html) is the board's source, with `ui/window.css`'s colours; the images are
rendered from it at 1280 px wide (`chromium-browser --headless --screenshot`, `?dark` for the dark
theme).
