# D11 — the window, drawn before it is built

**Drawn** 2026-10-13, second round · **Status**: shown to the decider; their word on the second
round is owed ([cycle 02, D11](../../cycles/02-the-audio-kept-as-recorded.md#d11)) · **Frozen**:
these drawings are not kept up to date. What the decider accepts becomes criteria of the cycle
that builds the window; the window built, and its screenshots, are then the truth. A new round
is a new folder.

The words on the boards are the product's, in French, as the decider read them.

| # | Board | What the user sees, and can do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The sessions](1-sessions-dark.png) · [light](1-sessions-light.png) | What the meetings weigh — audio as recorded against transcripts and minutes — and *Faire de la place*; the meetings by week, each with its date, length, what it holds (minutes, transcript, audio and its weight, or audio deleted); *Démarrer un enregistrement* and its shortcut |
| 2 | [One session](2-session-dark.png) · [light](2-session-light.png) | Who spoke over the meeting, here and remote; the minutes, with the model that wrote them and where, and *Refaire…*; the transcript, searchable, each line with its side and voice, with *Refaire…*; *Ouvrir le dossier* |
| 3 | [Freeing space](3-free-space-dark.png) · [light](3-free-space-light.png) | The two ways — the audio alone (transcript and minutes stay readable), or the whole meeting; the heaviest first, *Cocher celles de plus de 30 jours*; what is freed before it is done; everything goes to the system's trash, restored from there; without its audio a meeting cannot be transcribed again |
| 4 | [Settings](4-settings-dark.png) · [light](4-settings-light.png) | After a recording: nothing, the transcript, or the transcript then the minutes; per step, the model — on this computer, online with its address, or custom (address, model, key, test); the reminder to tell participants; when an application takes the microphone (nothing, offer to record, record alone) and stopping when it is let go; shortcut, folder, how long the audio is kept; the transcription model's state |
| 5 | [The two agreements](5-agreements-dark.png) · [light](5-agreements-light.png) | Before the first transcript: the model to download, its size, where from, checked on arrival. Before the first online minutes: exactly what leaves (the transcript) and what stays (the audio), to whom; *Ne plus demander pour ce service*; or write the minutes on this computer |
| 6 | [The small window, the offer, again](6-small-window-dark.png) · [light](6-small-window-light.png) | While recording: the time, the two sources' levels, *Arrêter*. A call seems to start: record, not this time, never offer or record alone. *Refaire…*: the model, and the current minutes stay until the new ones are ready |

They were drawn on a design canvas outside the repository; these images, rendered from it at
1280 px wide, are the record.
